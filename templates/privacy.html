Privacy

What Data Weaver stores, what it sends, and to whom.

What this server keeps

  • After you log in: your Wikimedia username, your user groups, and the OAuth tokens, encrypted, tied to a random session id in a cookie. They are deleted when you log out and expire after about two weeks.
  • No analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The server stores none of the files, captions or categories you work on; that data stays on Commons and in your browser.
  • As on any Toolforge tool, Wikimedia may keep standard web server logs under the Wikimedia Privacy Policy.

What your browser sends elsewhere

  • Commons and Wikidata receive your searches and file requests directly from your browser, as they would from any visitor.
  • Map tiles are loaded from the Wikimedia map server when you open a map.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (not run by Wikimedia) receives place names you type only if you switch on Search places on OpenStreetMap in the settings. It is off by default.

In your browser

Settings (language, typing tools, OpenStreetMap choice) are kept in your browser's local storage, and stars in session storage. Clear them in your browser, or use Reset settings.
